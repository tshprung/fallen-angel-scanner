# SFM: funding review and cash-flow hurdle

Reviewed October 29, 2026. Decision: keep as unresolved research; do not promote to a documented one-year doubling case on this evidence. This packet is separate from the active recovery-case registry.

## Funding evidence now checked

The [September-quarter 10-Q, Note 5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575515/000157551526000060/sfm-20260927.htm) reports an undrawn USD 600m revolver maturing July 25, 2030, and USD 22m of letters of credit reducing capacity. Nominal remaining capacity is USD 578m, subject to conditions. Quarter-end covenant compliance is reported; numeric current headroom is not established by this review. Management states expected liquidity covers at least twelve months.

This supports a dated funding assessment, not a complete 24-month repayment clearance. The quarter also contains substantial leases and other finance obligations. Neither the current portion nor the balance-sheet present value supplies the full future contractual payment calendar. Current lease additions, purchase commitments and financing terms still need reconciliation.

The [credit agreement, sections 1.01, 1.04, 4.02, 5.04 and 6.11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1575515/000157551525000216/ex101sprouts-jpmxcreditagr.htm) sets a 3.75x leverage ceiling and 3.00x cash-interest coverage floor, with conditional acquisition flexibility. Cash netting, debt/lease classification and pro-forma EBITDA differ from provider ratios. Borrowing requires representations to remain valid and no continuing default. Compliance computations are delivered to lenders; a blank certificate form is not a completed calculation. Consequently zero revolver drawings cannot establish zero contractual leverage or unconditional access to all remaining commitments.

## Temporary disruption versus ongoing business weakness

The [FDA's outbreak update](https://www.fda.gov/food/outbreaks-foodborne-illness/investigation-multistate-outbreak-cyclospora-illnesses-iceberg-lettuce-july-2026) says the outbreak ended September 11. [Sprouts' September 2 FAQ](https://www.sprouts.com/about/produce-safety/) states its lettuce was not implicated. Management's reference to disruption must not be turned into a claim that Sprouts sold recalled product.

An outbreak ending is not evidence that company traffic has recovered. A recovery thesis needs actual customer and margin evidence, while separating broad consumer weakness, pricing investment and new-store growth. Upcoming statements can test that thesis; no unannounced reporting date or guaranteed recovery is assumed.

|---|---:|

| Share repurchases, excluding excise tax | (429.222) |
| Proxy after those repurchases | (92.786) |

This is a cash-flow proxy, not verified distributable cash or a forward forecast. Operating lease payments are already reflected in operating cash flow: deducting the entire lease present value again would mix an equity cash-flow valuation with a different balance-sheet method. Other obligations and commitments remain to be checked. Growth investment cannot be removed while assuming its complete growth benefit.

Recent repurchases exceeded this proxy. They are discretionary, so unchanged spending should not be assumed in a stress case. The analysis neither assumes future buybacks create free value nor treats them as unavoidable debt service.

## Conditional stress, not a covenant calculation

Hold annual gross investment and identified finance principal fixed at those historical levels. Apply illustrative shocks to operating cash flow:

| OCF shock | Proxy before buybacks, USD m | Proxy if recent annual buybacks continued, USD m |
|---|---:|---:|
| None | 336.4 | (92.8) |
| Down 30% | 128.3 | (300.9) |
| Down 50% | (10.4) | (439.6) |

These mechanical shocks omit additional working-capital, pricing, legal, commitment and financing effects; they are not probabilities or a liquidity forecast. They show why capital-allocation flexibility matters. They do not establish how much the lender would permit the company to draw or a verified runway.

## What doubling requires

Use the dated October 28 reference price of USD 62, the reported September 27 share count of 92.673774m and an illustrative 5% share-count increase. This reported share snapshot is not a verified current diluted share count. Multiples below are assumptions, not a researched peer valuation. No probability is assigned.

| Annual equity cash-flow assumption, USD m | 10x | 15x | 20x | 25x |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 250 | $25.69 | $38.54 | $51.38 | $64.23 |
| 336.436 | $34.57 | $51.86 | $69.15 | $86.44 |
| 450 | $46.25 | $69.37 | $92.49 | $115.61 |
| 600 | $61.66 | $92.49 | $123.32 | $154.15 |

At 20x, doubling the reference price requires approximately USD 603.3m of annual equity cash generation after financing and required investment: 79.3% above the reconciled proxy. This is a reverse hurdle, not a forecast or an adopted recovery scenario. The earlier EPS sensitivity cannot establish that cash conversion improves enough to meet it. Reaching the hurdle at a higher multiple still needs a defensible multiple and growth case.

## Conditions for reconsideration

Reconsider only with evidence of sustained traffic/comparable-sales recovery, stable or recovering margins, improving cash generation after investment, and funding sufficient for the contractual payment calendar. Verify current covenant computations or an adequately supported contractual reconstruction, relevant listing/corporate-action risks, and the modeled share base. Check whether new stores earn acceptable returns without a continuing deterioration in existing-store demand.

Weaken or reject the recovery thesis if traffic remains weak, pricing materially damages margins, expansion fails to convert to cash, capital allocation consumes financing flexibility, or funding/listing concerns emerge. Set a fresh dated review horizon when a sourced catalyst and forward operating assumptions exist. No prospective milestone has been verified here as sufficient to support doubling within a year.

The research result is narrower than financial clearance: funding evidence improved, but the upside case remains unproven. Further work on SFM should be driven by new operating/funding evidence rather than repeated reinterpretation of the same quarter. BIRK remains a separate research priority.
