# LZB and ASBIS: further recovery and funding review

Reviewed October 22, 2026. Neither issuer has been admitted or given a completed recovery record. Prices below are the October 21 observations used in the completed universe scan, not current executable quotes. Companion calculations are in `2026-10-22-sensitivity.json`. No holdings, email or paper positions were changed by this research.

## La-Z-Boy: a real operating disappointment, with a possible recovery path

The largest daily fall in the reviewed August 1–October 21 adjusted-close series was approximately 16.95% on August 19, immediately after the August 18 results. Timing supports an earnings-related explanation, but does not identify every seller's motive or explain the entire subsequent decline. The June outlook was sales of USD 490–510m and adjusted operating margin of 4.0–5.5%; the August result was USD 475.689m and 3.9%. This is evidence of an operating miss, rather than solely an accounting charge. [June outlook](https://ir.la-z-boy.com/2026-06-16-La-Z-Boy-Incorporated-Reports-Strong-Fourth-Quarter-Results-Led-By-Retail-Sales-Growth-And-Broad-Based-Margin-Improvement-Finalizes-Multiple-Strategic-Initiatives), [August results](https://ir.la-z-boy.com/2026-08-18-La-Z-Boy-Incorporated-Reports-First-Quarter-Results-Retail-Momentum-With-Positive-Written-Same-Store-Sales).

The August release attributes the weakness to uneven wholesale orders, lower delivered volume and expense deleverage; plant-closure charges explain part of the GAAP/adjusted difference. Positive retail written same-store sales are encouraging, but orders need to convert into profitable deliveries. The issuer's next-quarter outlook of USD 500–520m sales and 4.0–5.5% adjusted margin includes ongoing investment and friction costs. These are dated management expectations, not achieved results.

The [October investor presentation, pages 4, 6 and 11](https://filecache.investorroom.com/mr5ir_lazboy/612/LZB-Investor_Presentation_vF.pdf) gives adjusted trailing EPS of USD 3.00 and describes double-digit enterprise operating margins as a long-term objective. Distribution improvements span multiple years. It does not substantiate achievement of those margins within twelve months. The [October 15 store announcement](https://ir.la-z-boy.com/2026-10-15-La-Z-Boy-Incorporated-Announces-Additional-New-Store-Openings-And-Acquisitions) confirms two New Orleans acquisitions; expansion consumes capital and does not establish an immediate earnings recovery.

### Funding review advanced, not cleared

The [annual report, Note 6 and liquidity discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/57131/000005713126000019/lzb-20260425.htm) reports lease payments of USD 112.039m within one year and USD 104.058m in the following year, measured from April 25, 2026, including finance leases. These are undiscounted payments, not the current-liability carrying value; their buckets are not an October 2026–October 2028 maturity ladder. Future-start operating leases add commitments outside the main table. FY2027 capex guidance is USD 90–110m. Do not subtract operating rent again from an OCF proxy already after rent when constructing cash coverage.

The [July-quarter report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/57131/000005713126000035/lzb-20260725.htm) confirms no revolver borrowings and covenant compliance as of July 25. The USD 541.511m provider debt field reconciles to current and long-term lease liabilities, rather than bank loans. July-period evidence still needs reconciliation with the provider's July 31 labels.

The [July 2025 amended credit agreement, section 9.9](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/57131/000005713125000054/exhibit41-amendedcreditagr.htm) requires net lease-adjusted leverage no greater than 3.50x and fixed-charge coverage no lower than 1.50x. A qualifying acquisition can permit a temporary 4.00x leverage limit under stated conditions; it is not the ordinary limit. The original HTML redline formatting was checked: 3.75 and 1.75 are deleted, while 4.00 and 1.50 are additions. Flattened extraction concatenates these values and would misstate the agreement. Covenant EBITDAR and fixed charges have contractual definitions; the scanner's EBIT/interest ratio is not covenant headroom. Current compliance certificates and post-quarter acquisition effects remain unverified.

### What doubling requires

At USD 28.68, doubling means USD 57.36 before dividends. That requires future diluted EPS of USD 5.736 at 10x earnings, USD 4.780 at 12x, or USD 3.824 at 15x. These multiples are illustrative requirements, not verified peer valuations. Compared with adjusted trailing EPS of USD 3.00, the 12x route needs roughly 59% earnings growth; adjusted EPS and GAAP EPS are different measures.

The existing generic FCF model requires about USD 120.6m normalized equity cash flow at 20x after assumed 5% dilution, versus USD 101.7m currently modeled. Alternatively, unchanged modeled cash flow needs 23.7x. The generic bull's near-2x result therefore relies on both cash-flow growth and a higher valuation multiple. No reviewed source establishes either within a year. Its bear sensitivity remains approximately -45%, not a loss bound.

Research milestones: subsequent results should demonstrate conversion of retail orders, wholesale margin recovery and cash generation after capex. Continued weak adjusted margins, rising cash use, or tighter funding would undermine the hypothesis. These are research conditions, not issuer promises. Keep the existing risk-score exclusion and missing recovery evidence in place.

## ASBIS: growth is established; the doubling and funding case is unfinished

The [October 20 issuer release](https://investor.asbis.com/news/investor-news/asbis-tops-usd-1bn-monthly-sales) reports preliminary September revenue of USD 1.052bn, up 216%, with strong server/component demand. Monthly sales are not profit, collections or a forecast. [October 19 market reporting](https://comparic.pl/akcje-asbis-traca-105-wyprzedaz-spolek-ai-na-wall-street-dotarla-na-gpw/) attributes the selloff to AI-sector sentiment after an earlier rally. That is a secondary-source interpretation, not proof that the decline is temporary or that customers remain creditworthy.

### Funding composition matters

The [H1 statements, Notes 17–19](https://investor.asbis.com/download/ASBISc_FS_H1_2026.pdf), in USD millions, reconcile as follows:

| Obligation at June 30 | Amount | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Overdrafts | 80.484 | Included in current borrowings |
| Short-term bank loans | 94.846 | Included in current borrowings |
| Current leases | 8.140 | Included in current borrowings |
| Factoring creditors | 64.991 | Included in current borrowings; do not add twice |
| Total current borrowings | 248.461 | Sum of the four rows above |
| Long-term bank loans | 13.172 | Part of long-term borrowings |
| Long-term leases | 34.280 | Part of long-term borrowings |
| Supplier-factoring liabilities | 69.995 | Separately presented current obligation |

Disclosed overdraft, short-term/revolving and factoring facility totals are USD 141.004m, 170.347m and 156.868m. Subtracting balances from limits does not establish committed undrawn liquidity: borrowing bases, collateral, renewal, expiry and draw conditions need verification. The facilities have security over assets/receivables/inventory and pledged deposits. No complete lender-by-lender maturity schedule or numeric covenant headroom was located in the reviewed H1 documents. That is an unresolved evidence gap, not evidence of a breach.

The [H1 management report, credit-risk discussion](https://investor.asbis.com/download/2026_Q2_ASBIS_EN_report_Q2_andH1_2026.pdf) explains that supplier obligations generally remain payable even when customers do not pay, and notes uninsured Ukraine exposure. Fast sales growth can therefore increase funding needs. The review still needs current collections, debtor concentration, insurance exclusions and cash accessibility; a low net-debt ratio alone is insufficient.

### External valuation cross-check does not yet support 2x

The issuer-hosted [August 18 Argus report](https://investor.asbis.com/download/Argus_Sponsored_ASB_AUG26.pdf) is sponsored research. It gives PLN 172 fair value using 16x estimated 2026 earnings and forecasts USD 212.3m net income for calendar 2027 in its financial table. Its assumptions predate September sales. These are analyst forecasts, not management guidance or independently verified future earnings. PLN 172 is only 12.8% above the scan's PLN 152.50 observation.

Using that USD 212.3m earnings figure, 55.5m initial shares, the scan's fixed FX and assumed 5% dilution produces about PLN 226 at 16x (+48%) or PLN 282 at 20x (+85%). Neither doubles. Doubling after that dilution requires USD 286.6m earnings at 16x, USD 229.2m at 20x, or 21.6x the USD 212.3m forecast. These are derived requirements, not recommendations; calendar-2027 full-year earnings also extend beyond a twelve-month horizon starting October 22, 2026. A price can capitalize forward earnings, but that timing and multiple need a separate argument.

The [November 5 scheduled Q3 report](https://investor.asbis.com/info/events-calendar) should help test margins, collections and financing behind growth. Merely having a reporting date is not a recovery catalyst. Keep ASBIS outside selected and documented-case lists; generic historical FCF normalization remains a limitation requiring explanation, not automatic replacement with peak cash flow.
