# PNR: acquisition funding and operating update

Reviewed October 29, 2026. PNR remains on hold; no recovery case or selection is created.

## Funding: one question resolved

The [October 27 quarterly filing, Note 10](https://pentair.gcs-web.com/node/27226/html) confirms the full USD 1.4bn acquisition-loan draw on October 1. USD 400m matures April 1, 2028; USD 1bn matures May 5, 2030, with installments beginning in Q1 2028. The former bridge commitment was replaced, so adding it again would double-count financing.

This resolves whether the committed acquisition loans were actually drawn. Current combined liquidity, covenant headroom, acquisition accounting and the complete 24-month repayment bridge remain unverified by this review.

## Operating evidence: recovery still needs proof

The [October 27 earnings release](https://investors.pentair.com/news-releases/news-release-details/pentair-reports-third-quarter-2026-results) says channel inventory realignment is complete. This is management's assessment; it does not establish sustained end-demand recovery. Pool quarterly sales fell 18% and segment income fell 38% year over year. Group adjusted EPS was USD 1.18 versus USD 1.24; the full-year adjusted EPS midpoint remained USD 4.70.

Quarterly operating cash flow was USD 169.3m; issuer-defined free cash flow was USD 152.7m versus USD 178.9m a year earlier. Results included approximately USD 8m of tariff refunds, which should not be extrapolated as recurring operating growth.

Interpretation: the disclosure advances the funding review and supports testing the destocking hypothesis, but does not establish a one-year doubling valuation or complete financial resilience clearance. Next reconcile combined funding and recurring cash generation, then build a sourced bear/base/recovery case with an explicit catalyst and invalidation conditions.
