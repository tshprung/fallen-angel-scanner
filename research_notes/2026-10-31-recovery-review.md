# Recovery decision review — 31 October 2026

No issuer is admitted to the documented-case registry by this review. This is a dated decision record with concrete blockers, not a list of approved purchases. The mandate is plausible +50% or more in twelve months, a comparable higher historical price, and financial/business resilience assessed through five years. Survival and preservation of principal cannot be guaranteed.

## SANM: cash conversion matters as much as reported growth

The June 27 quarterly filing reports a $2.2bn principal schedule: remainder FY2026 $86m; FY2027 $172m; FY2028/2029 $312m each; FY2030 $444m; FY2031 $242m; later $632m. The Term Loan A final maturity is October 27, 2030. Covenant thresholds are 3x cash-interest coverage and 4x net leverage; reported compliance does not establish current numerical headroom. Cash designated for Indian operations is $248m. Receivable sales affect operating cash flow. ZT contributed approximately $1.1bn of quarterly revenue, so reported growth is not organic growth. [Quarterly filing, debt, acquisition and liquidity sections](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/897723/000089772326000037/sanm-20260627.htm).

The July release guided FY2026 non-GAAP EPS of $11.90–$12.20 and discussed demand growth through FY2027/2028. That is an operating outlook, not a one-year share-price commitment. [Issuer results](https://ir.sanmina.com/news/news-details/2026/Sanmina-Reports-Third-Quarter-Fiscal-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

The mechanical stress uses provider TTM OCF $901.056m and PP&E $306.908m, with no new financing, no acquisitions, paused buybacks and Indian designated cash excluded. It reserves $1.568bn of principal through FY2031, including the already-ended FY2026 remainder conservatively. A sustained 50% OCF reduction leaves approximately $747m **before omitted needs**; a 70% reduction produces a $154m shortfall. These are static arithmetic scenarios, not cash forecasts or covenant calculations. Contingent consideration, changing investment needs, supplier/factoring availability, equity-tax payments and legal losses could worsen them. Do not subtract lease rent or interest again if already captured in GAAP OCF.

At the dated market cap, +50% after illustrative 5% dilution requires about $699m annual equity cash flow at 20x. This is a hurdle, not an estimate. The provider after-interest-buffer proxy is approximately $500m. Acquired-business comparability makes the older three-year median an imperfect forward anchor, but replacing it with an optimistic run rate would not constitute evidence.

**Reconsider when:** new post-ZT results show repeatable cash conversion, receivable sales and working capital are reconciled, contractual headroom is established, and a twelve-month cash-flow case is independently supported. **Invalidate:** integration/working-capital deterioration consumes funding faster than the stress allows, covenant concerns emerge, or the recovery relies mainly on multiple expansion.

## NOW: recurring growth, newly material funding obligations

The September filing reports $2.393bn cash, $2.214bn current marketable securities and approximately $7.0bn total cash/securities. Funded principal includes $2.1bn commercial paper, $750m notes due 2028, $1.5bn due 2030 and $600m due August 2031; other notes mature later. The undrawn $3bn revolver expires April 2031. Commercial paper and its liquidity backstop must not be counted as two independent permanent funding sources. Acquisition spending was $9.167bn over nine months. Existing cloud/IT commitments and renewal/refinancing risk belong in the five-year review. [September quarterly filing, debt and liquidity notes](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1373715/000137371526000096/now-20260930.htm).

The October release reported subscription growth of 21%. Nine-month GAAP OCF less PP&E equals $2.750bn; issuer non-GAAP FCF is $3.121bn after adding back $371m of business-combination costs. Its 36% full-year FCF-margin guidance uses the issuer definition. Do not mix those measures or automatically treat every acquisition-related payment as nonrecurring. [Issuer release and reconciliation](https://newsroom.servicenow.com/press-releases/details/2026/ServiceNow-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx).

At the dated market cap, +50% with 5% dilution requires approximately $8.59bn annual equity cash at 20x, or $5.73bn at 30x. The provider trailing proxy after the additional interest buffer is $4.592bn. The latter route requires cash growth and a higher multiple; 30x is an assumption requiring support, not a peer-derived target. A twelve-month thesis still needs a verified explanation for the selloff, specific operating milestones and a downside valuation.

**Reconsider when:** the next outlook supports actual cash after investment/financing, acquired operations meet measurable milestones, and funded maturities plus commitments are reconciled under a demand slowdown. **Invalidate:** growth/cash conversion weakens while acquisition financing and commercial-paper dependence rise.

## WDC: refinancing and dilution precede any target

The October 2 filing reports cash $1.314bn and debt principal $791m, including a $650m revolver expiring January 2027. Remaining convertibles have a November 16 redemption notice; earlier nominal 2028 maturity is not the operative near-term checkpoint. Conversion premiums were largely settled with shares, and the remaining conversion price is about $37.70; capped-call protection ends near $50.38. Principal, carrying value, premium and dilution are different quantities. Quarterly FCF was $1.377bn, alongside $1.001bn repurchases and $317m equity-tax withholding. Pausing distributions materially changes funding stress. [Quarterly filing, debt and cash flows](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000162828026066933/wdc-20261002.htm).

The issuer's next-quarter $5.80 non-GAAP EPS and 60.5% gross-margin midpoint guidance describes one quarter, not a four-quarter forecast. [October results](https://investor.wdc.com/node/28921). Older combined-company cash flows and prices require separation adjustments; the annual report explicitly identifies discontinued-operation cash-flow comparability issues. [Annual filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/106040/000162828026057139/wdc-20260703.htm).

The +50% hurdle is roughly $10.79bn annual cash at 20x or $7.19bn at 30x, versus a $4.172bn provider trailing buffered proxy. Thus good current liquidity does not by itself make the requested return attractive. Verify actual conversion settlement and revolver disposition before promoting this lead.

## TTD: an important model mismatch, not automatic clearance

The June filing shows $1.123bn cash and $362m short-term investments; the $750m revolver was undrawn apart from $5m letters of credit and matures April 14, 2031. Operating leases total $434.110m, matching the provider's reported total-debt figure. This explains why a generic interest-expense check can remain unverified despite no drawn revolver; it does not permit silently replacing missing interest with zero. Half-year OCF was $545.399m, PP&E $125.966m and capitalized software $7.399m. Cash conversion includes collection/payment timing and seasonality. [Quarterly filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1671933/000167193326000086/ttd-20260630.htm).

Quarterly revenue growth was only about 3%; the next-quarter revenue floor was $650m. An old high cannot establish that competitive pressure is temporary. [Second-quarter release](https://investors.thetradedesk.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/The-Trade-Desk-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx). The issuer confirmed November 9 after-close results. [Announcement](https://investors.thetradedesk.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/The-Trade-Desk-Announces-Date-of-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-and-Conference-Call/default.aspx).

**Decision:** watch the next results before underwriting a recovery. Stock compensation, capitalized development, advertising working capital and continuing competitive relevance must be reconciled. The generic long-decline and missing-interest exclusions remain active; no reviewed source override was introduced.
