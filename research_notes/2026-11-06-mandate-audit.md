# Investment mandate audit and two-year extension

The user confirmed that supported potential of at least +50% within one year
or at least +100% within two years are acceptable research objectives. A +50%
six-month case also qualifies. These are acceptable outcomes to the user,
not mathematically equal annualized returns or promised investment outcomes.

## Findings before the change

- Solvency checks and sourced five-year funding/resilience review already
  existed. They were not calibrated survival probabilities.
- Company-specific bear/base/recovery valuations, dilution, catalysts and
  invalidation already existed. Higher historical prices were context only.
- Competitive/technology risks were mentioned in the broad five-year review,
  but had no separate required customer-demand, competitive-position or
  technology-relevance blocks.
- A documented case could not have a target beyond 365 days. Paper tracking
  likewise always ended at one year.
- SPY comparisons existed for USD paper observations. There was no mandatory
  case-level opportunity-cost rule or measurable near-term progress checkpoint.

## Changes

Policy v5 retains the financial exclusions, positive current earnings/cash
requirements, recent-decline discovery filters, five-year resilience review,
official filing requirements and source freshness. It adds the accepted
two-year valuation horizon: +50% up to 365 days, +100% on days 366-730.
183/365/730-day bands separate documented 6/12/24-month cases in admission,
ranking, email, report ticker lists and paper tracking. The generic cash-flow
sensitivity remains research only and has not been converted into a forecast.

Five distinct sourced business reviews are now mandatory for documented cases:
customer need; competitive position; technology relevance and substitution;
pricing power/unit economics; and temporary versus structural decline.
Each must record the supporting finding and adverse risks, not merely assert
that the company survives or once traded at a higher price. A URL alone does
not establish that the review's conclusions are true.

Cases require a dated progress checkpoint no more than 90 days ahead, with a
measurable metric and invalidation condition. They also require a sourced
opportunity-cost review naming a comparator and a reassessment rule. Expired
checkpoints block documented status. These are research requirements; the
software does not read future earnings automatically, determine whether every
milestone succeeded, execute trades, or guarantee that waiting is worthwhile.

Reports expose total and annualized scenario returns, target dates, business
risks and checkpoint rules. Reverse sensitivities show both one-year +50% and
two-year +100% cash-flow hurdles; neither supplies timing evidence. The
declared research comparator is distinct from automated paper benchmarking,
which still supports only same-session USD SPY comparisons.

Paper performance method v4 records elapsed time and annualized comparisons,
supports all three horizon bands and exits new documented observations at
their actual immutable valuation deadline. A 90-day case is not given another
93 days merely because it is in the six-month band. Legacy observations keep
their original 365-day endpoint; old selection snapshots are never upgraded
or extended to rescue a losing outcome. Existing metrics are rechecked under
the new method using those original horizons.

The real recovery registry remains empty. No ticker was admitted, no research
hold was cleared and no portfolio/trade was created by changing this schema.
Sourced issuer underwriting remains the next substantive investment task.

## Validation

387 offline tests passed. New regressions cover return/horizon boundaries,

counterevidence, expired/late checkpoints, missing opportunity-cost rules,
unchanged filing/solvency exclusions, policy validation, all documented statuses
