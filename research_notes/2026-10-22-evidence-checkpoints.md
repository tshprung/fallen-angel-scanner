# Evidence checkpoints for the next company reviews

Reviewed October 22, 2026. None of these companies has a completed, admitted twelve-month recovery case. These are evidence requirements, not scheduled trades, price targets or automated risk clearances. Earlier price observations and generic sensitivities remain dated research inputs.

## What could be resolved today

**Pentair:** the [September 22 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/77360/000007736026000050/pnr-20260921.htm) appoints Robert W. Hau as CFO effective November 1. It is not a reconciliation of acquisition funding. The [September 18 filing](https://investors.pentair.com/node/27146/html) concerns a director appointment. Neither supplies the actual post-close term-loan draws or cash balance. The PNR hold remains PENDING; the CFO finding has been added to its sourced notes without clearing it.

**LZB:** the [July-quarter financial statements, Note 10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/57131/000005713126000035/lzb-20260725.htm) report USD 91.989m customer deposits alongside USD 267.254m cash. Their difference is USD 175.265m. This arithmetic is not a measure of unrestricted cash: deposits are obligations to customers, not automatically legally restricted bank balances. It demonstrates why gross cash cannot simply be treated as excess cash available for shareholder returns or all covenant calculations. July compliance is a dated issuer statement, not today's measured headroom.

The [credit agreement, sections 8.2 and 9.9 and the defined terms](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/57131/000005713125000054/exhibit41-amendedcreditagr.htm) requires completed compliance certificates to be delivered to lenders. An agreement's form of certificate does not supply the latest calculated ratios. The leverage calculation includes operating leases, limits the cash offset and deducts customer deposits in determining that offset. Its EBITDAR and fixed-charge definitions also differ from provider EBIT/interest. No completed current covenant calculation was located in the public documents reviewed. We cannot substitute a convenient provider ratio or assume all USD 200m of nominal facility capacity is immediately drawable.

The remaining funding questions are therefore specific evidence gaps. They do not establish a default, but do prevent current funding clearance. Additional repeated searches of the same dated filings cannot resolve an undisclosed post-close balance or a missing current compliance calculation.

The [issuer's October 14 announcement](https://investors.pentair.com/news-releases/news-release-details/pentair-release-third-quarter-2026-earnings-and-host-investor) schedules Q3 results for October 27, 2026, before the NYSE opens. | Evidence to extract | What it must resolve |

| Segment earnings and guidance bridge | Separate operating recovery from tariff refunds, adjustments and acquisition contributions. Do not treat the reversal of a one-off cost as recurring growth. |

Keep the hold until these facts are reconciled. A positive earnings headline or a covenant-compliance statement alone does not satisfy all release conditions. Weaken the operating recovery hypothesis if destocking persists, end-demand deteriorates, cash generation weakens or financing proves less available than assumed. These are research judgments, not new mechanical screening thresholds.

| Evidence to extract | What it must resolve |

## Required decision after each update

Record the primary URL, publication date, financial period, currency/units, relevant note and unresolved questions. Recalculate financial checks and a company-specific bear/base/recovery case from the updated evidence. Assess the cause of the decline, a measurable catalyst and invalidation conditions, financing/listing/dilution risks and the one-year horizon. A future scheduled result, an empty search or an optimistic analyst scenario cannot replace those findings. Admission and existing holds remain unchanged until their documented conditions are met.
